# Benchmark table notes (allocation rules, caveats)

ALL numbers were read directly from official MEE PDFs downloaded from mee.gov.cn (accessible from this session). Key structural notes:

(1) TERMINOLOGY BREAK: 2019-2022 benchmarks are 供电基准值 (per MWh of NET electricity supplied); from the 2023-2024 plan onward the metric is 发电基准值 (per MWh of GROSS generation). The ~0.82→~0.795 drop from 2022 to 2023 is therefore partly a denominator change, not comparable one-for-one. Unit-type official wording is 300MW等级以上 (above) vs 300MW等级及以下 (at-or-below) — the 及 attaches to the smaller class in every plan.

(2) 平衡值: introduced in the 2021-2022 plan (published only for 2021) and continued in the 2023-2024 plan (published only for 2023). 2022 benchmarks were derived from 2021 balance values minus targeted cuts; 2024 benchmarks = 2023 benchmarks × (1-0.5%). In 2023, coal benchmarks were set 0.4-0.8% BELOW the balance value while GAS benchmarks were set ~2.0% ABOVE it (0.3239 balance → 0.3305 benchmark). Heat balance values: 2021 coal 0.1110, gas 0.0560; 2023 coal 0.1041, gas 0.0525. The 2025/2026 draft publishes no power-sector balance values (it uses 2024 benchmarks as base) but introduces sector-level 平衡值 for steel/cement/aluminum.

(3) 修正系数 structure: 2019-2020 and 2021-2022 coal formula: 配额 = 供电基准值×供电量×Fl×Fr×Ff + 供热基准值×供热量, with Fl cooling-mode (water-cooled=1, air-cooled=1.05; 2021-22 adds back-pressure units=1), Fr heat-supply (coal 1-0.22×供热比; gas 1-0.6×供热比), Ff load(output)-factor for conventional coal condensing units (F>=85%: 1.0; 80-85%: 1+0.0014×(85-100F); 75-80%: 1.007+0.0016×(80-100F); F<75%: 1.015^(16-20F)); gas units used only Fr. The 2023-2024 plan ABOLISHED cooling and heat-supply corrections, leaving a single 调峰修正系数 (peak-regulation) for conventional coal units, =1.015^(16-20F) below the load-factor threshold of 65% (others=1). The 2025/2026 DRAFT lowers the compensation threshold to F<50% and replaces the formula with [7.254-0.633×(1-e^(-F/29.822))-5.643×(1-e^(-F/6.871))], =1 for F>=50%.

(4) Shortfall cap (配额履约缺口率20%上限): present in every plan. 2019-2020: if the gap exceeds 20% of verified emissions, surrender obligation is capped at free allocation + 20% of verified emissions; gas units never surrender more than their free allocation. 2021-2022: entity-level top-up so 核定配额 >= 80% of verified emissions; gas-unit exemption (应发配额 topped up to verified emissions); plus a flexibility mechanism letting entities with gap >=10% borrow (预支) up to 50% of the gap from 2023 pre-allocation — borrowing was discontinued afterward. 2023-2024 and the 2025/2026 draft keep the machine-level gas exemption and the entity-level 20% gap cap via 补发配额.

(5) Pre-allocation: 2019-2020: 70% of 2018 supply (supplied power/heat) × benchmark; 2021-2022: each year pre-allocated = 70% of the unit's 2021 VERIFIED EMISSIONS; 2023-2024: 70% of previous year's verified emissions; 2025/2026 draft: provincial discretion with a CAP of 50% of previous year's verified emissions (a cut from 70%). The 2023-2024 plan also introduced banking rules (2019-2024 vintage carryover into 2025 allowances, max = 10,000 tCO2 + 1.5 × net sales, applications through 2026-06-10; unbanked old vintages become unusable).

(6) 2025 POWER BENCHMARKS STATUS AS OF AUG 2026: NOT yet final. MEE released the combined 《全国碳排放权交易市场2025、2026年度发电行业以及2026年度钢铁、水泥、铝冶炼行业配额总量和分配方案（征求意见稿）》 for comment on 2026-07-27 (comments closed 2026-08-05); the plan was 审议并原则通过 at the MEE executive (部务) meeting chaired by Minister Huang Runqiu on 2026-08-24, but the final text had not been published as of 2026-08-26. Draft comprehensive power benchmark declines ~0.35%/yr in 2025 and 2026 vs 2024; the 2025/2026 rows above are DRAFT values and could change marginally in the final. For the Shandong spot-market paper, the binding 2024 values (0.7910/0.8049/0.8244/0.3288) are the latest FINAL benchmarks.
