# Event list notes (date conventions, exclusions)

Methodology: every event except the last was verified by fetching a dated primary or near-primary source (MEE/gov.cn/Xinhua/People's Daily pages showing 发布日期/生成日期/成文日期, or dated news articles quoting the release date). 'date' is the first date the information was publicly available (consultation-draft posting, State Council readout, Xinhua release, or MEE web posting), not the document's 成文日期, except as noted. Key date reconciliations vs the task's seed list: (1) the Interim Regulations were PASSED at the State Council executive meeting on 2024-01-05 (readout public same day), signed 2024-01-25 (not publicly known until publication), and the full text was released by Xinhua on 2024-02-04, effective 2024-05-01 — use 2024-01-05 and 2024-02-04 as tradable dates, not 01-25; (2) the industrial-sector allocation plan is dated 2025-11-16 (成文) but MEE shows 发布日期 2025-11-17 with web posting 11-18 — 2025-11-17 used; (3) the final 2023/2024 power allocation notice is dated 2024-10-15/16 but first appeared publicly with MEE Q&A and exchange notices on 2024-10-21 — 2024-10-21 used, so treat the 10-16/10-21 gap cautiously in daily event studies; (4) the 2025-08-25 中办/国办 Opinions document is dated 2025-05-24 but was only released 2025-08-25. The 2021-10-26 settlement notice (成文 10-23) is the announcement event for the 2021-12-31 deadline — both included; likewise deadline events (2023-12-31, 2024-12-31, 2025-12-31, and 2026-12-31 announced in the 2026-02-09 notice) are anticipated once their notices publish, so in a shock series treat notice dates as the information events and deadline dates as seasonal/liquidity events. MEE monthly/annual market reports were excluded as instructed. Not included for lack of clean national-level release dates or materiality: provincial pre-allocation notices, the Aug 2024 consultation on second-batch CCER methodologies (final 2025-01-03 included), a third CCER methodology batch reported around late Nov 2025 (People's Daily 2025-12-01 'CCER市场再扩容' — could be added if needed), and the 2025-01 green-certificate/CCER interface notice. The 2026-08-24 approval event is included with date_verified=false; the final 2025/2026 allocation plan itself had not been published as of 2026-08-26 and should be appended when issued.
